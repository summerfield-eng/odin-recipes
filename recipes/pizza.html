Pizza

This delicious thin crust pizza dough recipe is so low maintenance, you can throw it together in just 15 minutes. How to Make Thin Crust Pizza Dough Thin Crust Pizza Dough Ingredients It’s easy to make this thin crust pizza dough recipe with a few ingredients you may likely already have on hand. Here’s what you’ll need: Yeast: This thin crust pizza dough starts with one teaspoon of active dry yeast. Sugar and water: Activate the yeast in white sugar and lukewarm water. Flour: This crispy thin crust calls for all-purpose flour instead of bread flour. Salt: Salt tightens the gluten structure, which strengthens the dough and makes it more elastic.

Ingredients

  • 1 teaspoon active dry yeast
  • ¼ teaspoon white sugar
  • ¾ cup lukewarm water
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour, divided
  • ½ teaspoon salt
